About the Application

Bookroom DBMS for Schools is a school-focused bookroom management system developed by Mona Technologies to support the efficient organization, administration, and tracking of school textbook inventories and rental activities.

The system is designed to streamline book registration, student records, book issuance and returns, and inventory management through a centralized digital platform. By reducing reliance on manual recordkeeping, Bookroom DBMS for Schools aims to improve operational efficiency, record accuracy, and accountability within school bookrooms.

This application represents the first official release (Version 1.0.0) of the Bookroom DBMS platform and establishes the foundation for future improvements, features, and system enhancements.
